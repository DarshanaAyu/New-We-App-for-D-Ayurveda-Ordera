📥 Export as CSV

Download your entire medicine catalogue as a CSV file. You can open it in Excel or Google Sheets, edit prices, and re-import it.

📋 CSV Format Reference

The CSV uses these columns. Quantity/price columns are flexible — any number of pairs are supported.

name,category,qty_1,price_1,qty_2,price_2,... Examples: Dhanwantaram,Kashaya,250g,1670,500g,3120,1Kg,5790 Triphala Churna,Churna,250g,320,500g,590,1Kg,1100 Brahmi Guli,Guli/Tablets,60 Qty,440,250 Qty,1670,1000 Qty,5790 Karpasasthyadi,Oils,100ml,580,200ml,990,500ml,2450,1L,4230,2L,8050
